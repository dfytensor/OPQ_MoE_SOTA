\section{Real-Model Validation}
Chapters 1--6 developed OPQ-MoE analytically. This chapter validates on
actual pre-trained weights.

\subsection{Setup}
Model: GPT-2 (124M). 50 linear layers. Baselines: FP16, RTN-8/4bit,
GPTQ-sim, AWQ-sim. OPQ variants: Global, PC, PC+SR, MoE-4b, MoE-3b.

\subsection{Key Results}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{OPQ-PC 4-bit: 99.554\% WtdRet, KLD$_w$=0.204}
\item GPTQ-sim 4-bit: 99.449\%, KLD$_w$=4.130
\item AWQ-sim 4-bit: 98.781\%, KLD$_w$=16.922
\item RTN 4-bit: 98.970\%, KLD$_w$=23.892
\item OPQ-PC KLD$_w$ is 20$\times$ lower than GPTQ, 100$\times$ lower than RTN
\item OPQ-MoE 3-bit: 96.678\% at 5.33$\times$ compression
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Correct SR Implementation}
SR must operate in quantization domain (Bernoulli on fractional part),
not weight domain. Incorrect v1 impl collapsed retention to 0.7\%;
corrected v2 achieves 99.1\%.

\subsection{Why OPQ Wins on KLD}
Power transform $v=(|w|/m)^\alpha$ maps Laplacian weights to near-uniform
quantized representation, making histograms nearly indistinguishable
from original (KLD 0.2 vs 23.9 for RTN).

\subsection{Limitations}
GPT-2 only; LLaMA-2-7B/Mixtral pending. GPTQ-sim/AWQ-sim simplified.
Custom inference kernel future work.
